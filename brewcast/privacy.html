Privacy Policy for Brewcast

Effective Date: November 2026 App: Brewcast — Caffeine Tracker (iPhone, iPad)

Short version: Brewcast does not collect any data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your caffeine log stays on your device and, if you keep iCloud sync on, in your own private iCloud.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Brewcast app for iPhone and iPad ("the App"), including its widgets, Live Activities and Siri / Shortcuts actions.

The developer does not receive, store or have access to anything you enter in the App.

2. What the App Stores and Where

To estimate the caffeine in your body, the App keeps:

  • the drinks you log (name, caffeine amount, volume and time);
  • your settings: sleep schedule, half-life, sensitivity options, daily limit, favourite drinks and reminders;
  • drinks you create yourself.

This data is stored on your device, in a container shared only with the App's own widgets. If iCloud sync is on (Profile → Data), it is also stored in your private iCloud database so your devices stay in sync. Apple operates iCloud; the developer cannot read your iCloud data.

3. Apple Health

  • With your permission, the App writes the caffeine of each drink you log to Apple Health as Dietary Caffeine, and removes it when you delete the drink.
  • The App does not read any data from Apple Health.
  • Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold and never shared with third parties.
  • You can turn this off in Profile → Data or in the Health app (Sharing → Apps → Brewcast).

4. Notifications

Reminders such as "Last cup" or "Ready for sleep" are scheduled on your device. No notification content is sent to a server.

5. Purchases

Brewcast Pro is sold through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; the App only receives confirmation from Apple that you have access to Pro. The developer never sees your payment details.

6. Analytics and Crash Reports

The App contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. If you choose in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide the developer with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics under Apple's own privacy policy.

7. Exporting and Deleting Your Data

  • Export: Profile → Data → Export CSV creates a file you can save or share. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to share it.
  • Delete: Profile → Data → Delete All Data removes your log, settings and the caffeine samples the App wrote to Apple Health, on this device and in iCloud.
  • Deleting the App removes its data from the device. iCloud data can also be removed in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

8. Health Disclaimer

Brewcast shows estimates, not medical advice. If you are pregnant or have a heart condition, agree on your caffeine limit with a doctor.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Caffeine tracker & sleep cutoff

Drink coffee. Sleep well.

Caffeine has a half-life of about five hours, so the afternoon latte is still with you at bedtime. Brewcast tracks the caffeine in your body from the drinks you log. It shows how much more fits today, and when your last cup stops affecting sleep.

Privacy Policy for WaterTotter

Effective Date: November 2026 App: WaterTotter — Water Tracker (iPhone, Apple Watch, Mac)

Short version: WaterTotter has no account, no server, no analytics and no ads. Your water log and profile stay on your device. The developer never sees them.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the WaterTotter app for iPhone, Apple Watch and Mac ("the App"), including its widgets and Watch complications.

The developer does not receive, store or have access to anything you enter in the App.

2. What the App Stores and Where

To track your water and calculate a daily goal, the App keeps:

  • the drinks you log (amount and time) and your daily history;
  • the profile you enter for the goal calculation: age, height, weight, sex, activity level and climate;
  • your settings: daily goal, units, reminders and notification sound, colors and the background image you pick.

This data is stored only on your device, in a container shared with the App's own widgets. It is not synced to iCloud or sent to any server. If you use the Apple Watch app, your progress and settings are passed directly between your iPhone and your Watch.

A background image is chosen through the system photo or file picker. The App only gets the image you select and keeps it on your device.

3. Apple Health

  • With your permission, the App writes each drink you log to Apple Health as Water.
  • The App does not read any data from Apple Health.
  • Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold and never shared with third parties.
  • You can turn this off at any time in the Health app (Sharing → Apps → WaterTotter). Water already saved to Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app.

4. Notifications

Reminders to drink water are scheduled on your device. No notification content is sent to a server.

5. Sharing the App

The App may offer to share WaterTotter on Instagram Stories, X, Facebook or through the iOS share sheet. Sharing only happens when you tap it, and what is shared is a link to the App on the App Store (and, for Instagram, a picture with a QR code to that link). Your log, profile and Health data are never shared.

To open the Facebook share dialog, the App includes Meta's Facebook SDK. Automatic app event logging and advertising ID collection are turned off in the App, and the App does not use it for analytics or ads. When the App starts, the SDK may still connect to Meta to load its settings, so Meta may receive technical information such as your IP address, device model and system version. That information is handled under Meta's Privacy Policy. Content you post to Instagram, X or Facebook is handled under those services' own policies.

6. Tips

Optional tips are sold through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; the App only receives confirmation from Apple that a purchase went through. The developer never sees your payment details.

7. Analytics and Crash Reports

The App contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. If you choose in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide the developer with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics under Apple's own privacy policy.

8. Deleting Your Data

  • Deleting the App removes its log, profile and settings from your device (and from your Apple Watch when the Watch app is removed).
  • Water the App saved to Apple Health is managed in the Health app, where you can delete it.
  • The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request or delete on a server.

9. Health Disclaimer

WaterTotter's daily goal is a general estimate, not medical advice. If a doctor has told you to limit or increase your fluid intake, follow their advice.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

11. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Support

Need a hand?

Is WaterTotter really free?

Yes. There are no subscriptions, no paywalls and no ads. The only in-app purchases are optional tips that unlock nothing — they simply support development.

What data do you collect?

None. Your logs and settings stay on your device, and water intake is only shared with Apple Health if you turn that on. Read the full Privacy Policy.

How is my daily goal calculated?

With Auto Calculate on, WaterTotter uses your age, height, weight, sex, activity level and climate. Prefer your own number? Turn Auto Calculate off and set the goal with the − / + buttons.

Does it work on Apple Watch?

Yes — there's a Watch app with complications (watchOS 10.6 or later). WaterTotter also runs on Macs with Apple silicon.

My reminders don't arrive. What should I check?

Make sure notifications are allowed for WaterTotter in iOS Settings, check that a Focus mode isn't silencing them, and confirm the reminder interval and start / end times in the app.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Send an email with your device model, iOS version and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot. Every message gets read.

Write to the developer

WaterTotter is made by one indie developer. Questions, ideas and bug reports are always welcome.

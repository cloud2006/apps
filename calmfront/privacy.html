Privacy Policy for Calmfront

Effective Date: November 2026 App: Calmfront — Barometer & Pressure Alerts (iPhone)

Short version: Calmfront does not collect any data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your location is used only to get the weather forecast from Apple, and your diary stays on your iPhone.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Calmfront app for iPhone ("the App"), including its Home Screen widgets.

The developer does not run any server for the App and does not receive, store or have access to your location, your diary or anything else you enter in the App.

2. Location

  • With your permission ("While Using the App"), the App reads your approximate position to show the air pressure forecast for where you are.
  • To get that forecast, your coordinates are sent to Apple Weather (WeatherKit). To show the name of your town, they are sent to Apple Maps for reverse geocoding. Both requests go directly from your iPhone to Apple and are handled under Apple's Privacy Policy. Apple does not share them with the developer.
  • The last known position, the town name and the latest forecast are kept on your device so the App, its widgets and background refresh can work without asking for your location again.
  • You can use the App without location. It then only shows the reading of your iPhone's own pressure sensor, with no forecast and no alerts. You can change the permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Calmfront.

3. Pressure Sensor (Motion & Fitness)

With your permission, the App reads the barometer built into your iPhone to show the pressure at your altitude. The reading is used only on the device and is not stored or sent anywhere.

4. Your Diary

When you log how you feel, the App stores the entry: the time, the symptom (for example migraine or joint pain), its intensity, an optional note, and the air pressure and pressure change at that moment.

  • Diary entries are stored only on your device. They are not synced to iCloud or sent to any server.
  • This information may relate to your health. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold and never shared with anyone.
  • The App does not read from or write to Apple Health.

5. Settings and Widgets

Your settings (pressure unit, alert sensitivity, quiet hours, theme and what bothers you) and a copy of the latest forecast are stored on your device, in a container shared only with the App's own widgets.

6. Notifications

Pressure-drop alerts are calculated and scheduled on your iPhone. No notification content is sent to a server, and the App does not use push notifications.

7. Purchases

Calmfront Plus is sold through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; the App only receives confirmation from Apple that you have access to Plus. The developer never sees your payment details.

8. Analytics and Crash Reports

The App contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. If you choose in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide the developer with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics under Apple's own privacy policy.

9. Deleting Your Data

  • Diary entries: touch and hold an entry in the Diary and choose Delete.
  • Everything: deleting the App removes all of its data from your device, including the diary, settings and the saved location.

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on the developer's side. You can still contact us with any privacy request.

10. Health Disclaimer

Calmfront shows weather information, not medical advice. It is not a medical device and does not predict or diagnose migraines or any other condition. Talk to a doctor about your symptoms and treatment.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

12. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, for example if optional iCloud sync is added, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

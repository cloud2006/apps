Support & FAQ

Need assistance, have a question, or want to suggest an improvement? Here is everything you need.

How do I restore past purchases?

Open the app settings, navigate to Purchases / Subscription, and tap Restore Purchases. Ensure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the initial purchase.

How is my personal data handled?

All personal logs, habits, and route data are stored directly on your device and synchronized via your personal Apple iCloud container. We do not operate user-tracking servers or sell personal data. You can read our full Privacy Policy for details.

How do I request a feature or suggest ideas?

I welcome all feedback and feature ideas from users! Drop an email to dev.pavlo.antoniuk@gmail.com. I read and respond to every message.

How do I report a bug?

Please send an email including your device model, iOS version, and a brief description of the steps that lead to the issue. A screenshot or screen recording is always extremely helpful.

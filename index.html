Support

Need help or want to suggest a feature? Check the quick answers below or send me a message.

How do I restore purchases?

Open the app, go to Settings → Purchases, then tap Restore. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy the app.

How do I request a new feature?

Send a short description of your idea using the email dev.pavlo.antoniuk@gmail.com. I read every message.

How do I report a bug?

Please include your device model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce. A quick screen recording helps a lot. email dev.pavlo.antoniuk@gmail.com.

Features

Everything App Store Connect does — faster.

Metadata, localization, screenshots, reviews and pricing for every app and every account, without opening the website.

Dashboard

See every app at a glance

  • Versions in review, rejected builds, unanswered reviews and average rating for all your apps.
  • “Needs Attention” lists what to do next: missing screenshots, approved builds waiting for release, low-star reviews.

Metadata & versions

Edit, review, sync — when you’re ready

  • Edit the name, subtitle, description, keywords, promotional text and What’s New for the live version and the one in preparation.
  • Work on local drafts and sync to App Store Connect when you’re ready, with every change listed before it’s sent.
  • Create a new version, choose the build and release an approved version without opening the website.

AI localization

Every language, without the busywork

  • Translate metadata into all your languages with Apple Intelligence on your Mac, or with a cloud AI provider using your own API key.
  • Review every translation before it’s synced; character limits are checked for each field.
  • Keywords are always yours to write per language.
  • Prefer your own workflow? Export a translation file, translate it anywhere and import it back.

Screenshot Studio

Design once, localize only the captions

  • Design App Store screenshots once: backgrounds, device layouts and text that fits.
  • Translate captions with AI and preview how your product page will look.
  • Upload to App Store Connect for every language and device size, including the latest iPhone displays.

Reviews & ratings

Every review from every country, in one list

  • Read and reply to customer reviews from every country, with AI-drafted replies you can edit.
  • Ratings by country with a star breakdown and a 30-day history, so you see changes as they happen.

In-app purchases & pricing

Products and fair prices for every country

  • See all subscriptions and in-app purchases with their status, prices and localizations.
  • Create and edit products, set prices and introductory offers.
  • Find out exactly what a “Missing Metadata” product still needs, and fix it in place.
  • Localized pricing: suggest fair prices for every country from purchasing-power indexes, review them and apply in one step.

Marketing Launchpad

Keep track of where you promote each app, log your posts and get a reminder when a channel’s cooldown is over.

Private by design

Your keys, your Mac, your data.

AppShipper talks directly to Apple using your own App Store Connect API key.

  • Keys stay in your KeychainYour App Store Connect API key is stored in your Mac’s Keychain.
  • No servers, no trackingThere are no AppShipper servers and no analytics. Requests go straight to Apple.
  • Cloud AI only if you chooseText goes to a cloud AI provider only if you pick one and add your own key.

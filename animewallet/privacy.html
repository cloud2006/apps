Privacy Policy for AnimeWallet

Effective Date: November 2026 App: AnimeWallet (iOS)

In short: AnimeWallet stores your lists, collections and profile in your account so they sync across devices and can be shared when you choose. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information the AnimeWallet iOS app ("the App", "we") collects, why, who helps us process it, and the choices you have. It applies to the App and to the pages it links to on antcraft.dev.

You can browse anime, charts, public collections and curator profiles as a guest, without an account. Features that save or share anything — your lists, collections, following curators — need an account.

2. Accounts & Sign-In

You can create an account or sign in with Sign in with Apple, Sign in with Google, or an email address and password.

  • Sign in with Google: Google shares your basic profile with us: your name, email address and profile picture. We use them only to create your account, sign you in and suggest a display name. We never receive your Google password and have no access to Gmail, Contacts, Drive or any other Google data.
  • Sign in with Apple: Apple shares your name (if you choose to) and your email address, or a private relay address if you hide it.
  • Email and password: we store your email address and a securely hashed password, and email you a code to confirm the address. Password resets work the same way.
  • Information received from Google or Apple is not sold, shared for advertising or used for anything other than your account. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy.

3. Information You Add to the App

When you have an account, the following is stored with it on our servers so it syncs across your devices:

  • Profile: username, display name, an optional bio and an optional profile photo you choose.
  • Lists: the anime you track, their status (watching, completed, planned and so on), episodes watched, your ratings, personal notes and the order you set.
  • Collections: their titles, descriptions, cover images, the anime in them and your notes on each.
  • Social activity: the curators you follow, collections you save, likes and dislikes, people you block, and collections shared with you.

Photos are picked with the iOS photo picker, so the App only ever receives the photo you select, never the rest of your library.

4. What Other People Can See

  • Your profile (username, display name, bio, photo, follower counts and the Curator badge if you have Premium) is visible to everyone who uses the App, including guests.
  • Your lists are shown on your profile by default: the anime, their status and progress. Your ratings and personal notes are never shown to anyone else. You can hide your lists in Settings → Privacy.
  • Collections are private when created. A public collection is visible to everyone and appears for your followers; a private one is visible only to you and the people you share it with by link or invitation. Cover images are stored at hard-to-guess addresses, so anyone given the exact address of a private collection's cover could open that image.
  • Blocking someone hides your profile, collections and lists from them and theirs from you.

5. Premium Subscriptions

Premium is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your card or payment details. To tie Premium to your account, the App sends Apple's signed transaction information to our server, which checks it with Apple and keeps a record of the subscription (product, renewal and expiry dates) linked to your account. Apple also notifies our server about renewals, cancellations and refunds.

6. Reports & Safety

If you report a profile or a collection, we keep the report — the reason, any details you add and a copy of what you saw — and email it to the developer for review. The person you report is not told who reported them. When enough people report a collection, it may be hidden automatically.

7. Usage Analytics & Diagnostics

  • To count how many people use the App, it records when it is opened: a random identifier created on install (reset if you reinstall), your account ID if you are signed in, and the App and iOS versions. We don't record your IP address, device model, location or advertising identifier.
  • If you have chosen to share analytics with developers in your iOS settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

8. Anime Information from AniList

Anime details, charts, studios and artwork come from AniList. The App requests them directly from AniList's servers, so AniList receives your device's network address and what you browse or search for in the catalog. No account information is sent to AniList. AniList's own privacy policy applies to those requests.

9. Service Providers

We use a small number of providers to run the App. They process information only on our behalf:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, accounts and image storage.
  • Resend — sends account emails (confirmation and password-reset codes) and report notifications to the developer.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases and crash reports.
  • Google — Sign in with Google.

These providers may store data in countries other than yours. We don't sell your information and only disclose it if required by law.

10. On Your Device

The App keeps your sign-in session in the iOS Keychain and a few preferences (such as appearance and how you last signed in) on your device, along with cached images and catalog data so screens load faster. Deleting the App removes them.

11. Keeping & Deleting Your Data

  • We keep your account data for as long as you have an account. You can edit or delete your profile, lists and collections at any time in the App.
  • Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your account, profile, lists, collections, photos, follows and subscription records. If you signed in with Apple or Google, it also asks them to revoke the App's access to that account. Usage records are kept without any link to you.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel it in your Apple Account settings.
  • You can also remove AnimeWallet's access from your Google Account connections or, for Apple, under Sign in with Apple in your Apple Account settings.
  • To ask for a copy of your data or for help with anything above, email us at the address below.

12. Children's Privacy

AnimeWallet is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. Updates are posted on this page with a new effective date.

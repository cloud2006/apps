Support

Questions, answered.

When will AnimeWallet be available?

AnimeWallet is in active development and coming soon to the App Store for iPhone. This page will get a download button the day it launches.

Where do the scores come from?

Community scores shown next to each title come from AniList, so you can see at a glance how a show is rated.

Who can see my lists?

You decide what you share with friends. AnimeWallet doesn't sell your data, doesn't track you across other apps and websites, and doesn't use your watchlists or reviews for advertising. Read the Privacy Policy.

Can I suggest a feature?

Please do! Ideas from anime fans shape what gets built next. Send an email — every message gets read.

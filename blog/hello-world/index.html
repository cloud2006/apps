Hello, world: welcome to the blog

antcraft.dev now has a blog. Here’s why, and what you can expect to read here.

Hi, I’m Pavlo — an indie developer from Ukraine 🇺🇦 building apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch. Until now this site was just a list of apps. But a lot happens between the first idea and an App Store release, and I’d like to share some of it.

What I’ll write about

  • Behind the scenes — how apps like HabitsFlow, WaterTotter and AppShipper are designed and built.
  • Releases — what’s new in each update and why it changed.
  • Indie development — lessons from shipping and supporting apps on my own.
  • Privacy — how I approach your data, and the trade-offs that come with it.

Say hello

Is there something you’d like me to write about? A feature you’re curious about, or a question about how an app works? Send me an email at support@antcraft.dev — I read and reply to every message.

Thanks for stopping by. More soon.

Privacy Policy for Pageglow

Effective Date: November 2026 App: Pageglow — Reading Light (iPhone and iPad)

Short version: Pageglow does not collect any data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your settings stay on your device.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Pageglow app for iPhone and iPad ("the App"), including its widgets, its Control Center control and its Shortcuts actions.

The developer does not run any server for the App and does not receive, store or have access to anything you do in the App.

2. What the App Does Not Use

The App does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data or any other personal information, and it does not need an internet connection to work.

3. Settings Stored on Your Device

To open straight to the light you used last, the App stores on your device:

  • the selected light mode, its brightness and warmth;
  • up to five colors you save (Plus);
  • your choices in Settings, such as the default mode and the sleep timer options;
  • whether Pageglow Plus is unlocked, so widgets and shortcuts know it.

This information is kept in a container shared only with the App's own widgets and controls. It is not synced to iCloud or sent anywhere.

4. Screen Brightness

While the lamp is on, the App changes your screen brightness and keeps the screen awake. It remembers the brightness you had before and puts it back when you leave the App. Nothing about your brightness is stored or sent anywhere.

5. Purchases

Pageglow Plus is a one-time purchase sold through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; the App only receives confirmation from Apple that you have access to Plus. The developer never sees your payment details or your Apple Account.

6. Siri and Shortcuts

If you use "Turn On Reading Light" with Siri, Shortcuts or Control Center, iOS runs the action on your device and passes only the chosen light mode to the App. Siri requests are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

7. Analytics and Crash Reports

The App contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. If you choose in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide the developer with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics under Apple's own privacy policy.

8. Deleting Your Data

  • Saved colors: touch and hold a saved color and choose Delete Color.
  • Everything: deleting the App removes all of its data from your device.

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on the developer's side. You can still contact us with any privacy request.

9. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

About me

I’ve been building apps for iOS since 2017. This site is home to my personal projects — apps I design, build and support on my own. In my work I put privacy first and keep your data stored locally on your device.

Outside of code, I’m into motorcycles 🏍️ and I’m raising two cats — so your app purchases and donations go to them first. 🐾

Your data stays yours

I build my apps with respect for your personal data. Unless it’s truly necessary, I don’t use any analytics tools and I don’t collect data about the people who use my apps.

In the rare cases where an app does rely on analytics, I say so openly in its Privacy Policy — no surprises, no fine print.

🔒

Private by default

Your data lives on your device and, if you choose, in your own iCloud — not on my servers.

📊

No analytics without need

No tracking or data collection unless an app genuinely needs it — and then it’s disclosed.
